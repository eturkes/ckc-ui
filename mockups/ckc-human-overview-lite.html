CKC makes research evidence you can replay.

The page starts with the smallest useful story. More detail stays one click away.

2

4

Publish the evidence

Claim boundary

This is formalization-QA evidence over declared sources and locked fixtures. It is not patient-care guidance, CDS runtime behavior, SaMD evidence, or regulatory approval. Stronger claims require explicit gate evidence.
