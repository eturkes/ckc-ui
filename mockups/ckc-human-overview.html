What CKC does

A compact answer for humans before the detailed workbench.

research harness

CKC turns guideline text into replayable formalization evidence.

The useful first screen is not a full architecture map. It is one source-grounded thread: a quoted span, the extracted meaning, the reusable rule, the machine check, and the report row that records a finding or a documented null result.

Visible boundary This page presents formalization-QA evidence over locked fixtures and artifacts. It does not present patient-care guidance, a CDS runtime, SaMD evidence, or regulatory approval.

Claim boundary

Humans need the blocked jumps as much as the successful path.

no shortcut

Missing gate evidence blocks only the stronger claim.

Lower-tier locked measurements remain useful. The UI should show the successful research evidence and the exact residual that prevents clinical, regulatory, permission, or self-improvement claims.
